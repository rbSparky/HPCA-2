# Consumer path

The final model reuses the unchanged routed eight-lane banked decoder/support-cache evidence, shares its achieved service resource between anchor and target decode, and charges finite memory/decoder contention. The Nangate45 ORFS route has 0 DRC errors, 0.565 ns positive slack at a 1.0 ns clock, 1,795 um2 standard-cell area, and 68,220 um2 die area. The result establishes an eight-lane cluster; wider scaling remains a modeled hierarchy and is not represented as routed evidence.
